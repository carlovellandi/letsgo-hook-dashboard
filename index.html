Method

Study flow

All five trial reels are compared through 48 hours. The winner is then promoted to the main feed, and the seven-day pull tracks its performance after promotion.

Step 1One source videoHold footage, length, edit, and packaging constant.
Step 2Five trial reelsCredential, numbered, POV, contrarian, and cold open.
Step 324h + 48h pullsRetention first; watch depth and engagement as supporting signals.
Step 4Promote 48h winnerExactly one winning trial reel moves to the main feed.
Step 57-day pullTrack the promoted winner with its added feed distribution in mind.
